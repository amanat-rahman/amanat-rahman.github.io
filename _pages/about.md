Amanat Ur Rahman is a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering in the Department of Mechanical, Materials, and Industrial Engineering at the University of Cincinnati, advised by [Dr. Tommaso Giovannelli](https://sites.google.com/view/tommasogiovannelli). His research develops stochastic optimization models and exact decomposition algorithms for large-scale scheduling systems that operate under uncertainty.

His dissertation addresses outpatient appointment scheduling in clinics where patient no-shows, short-notice cancellations, and walk-in arrivals make demand unpredictable. The work formulates two-stage stochastic programs with integer first-stage decisions covering overbooking, open-access slot allocation, and walk-in admission, and solves them through an integer L-shaped decomposition embedded in a branch-and-cut framework. A companion line of work extends the formulation to a multi-objective setting within a stochastic set-optimization framework, characterizing the trade-offs among competing operational objectives such as patient waiting time, provider idle time, and session overtime.

Beyond healthcare operations, his applied work spans airline network planning, semiconductor process control, and supply chain analytics. He has built scenario-based evaluation frameworks with mixed-integer recourse for cargo network planning, state-space filtering models for virtual metrology in chemical mechanical planarization, and multi-task survival models quantifying institutional variability in post-transplant outcomes.

Teaching is central to his professional identity. He served for six years on the faculty of the Department of Mechanical and Production Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology, where he taught sixteen distinct undergraduate courses and laboratories across operations research, production planning and control, supply chain management, engineering economy, and industrial simulation, and authored four standing laboratory manuals. He is currently on the academic job market for tenure-track and teaching-professor positions beginning Fall 2027.
