## Research experience

**Graduate Research Assistant**, University of Cincinnati, August 2023 to present
Department of Mechanical, Materials, and Industrial Engineering

- Two-stage stochastic programming models for outpatient appointment scheduling with overbooking, open access, and walk-in patients, solved by an integer L-shaped decomposition algorithm in Gurobi and Python
- Extension to a multi-objective two-stage stochastic model within a stochastic set-optimization framework, characterizing the Pareto frontier across competing operational objectives
- Dual linear Kalman filter virtual metrology model for material removal rate prediction in chemical mechanical planarization, attaining the lowest mean squared error against multiple linear regression, EWMA, and Bayesian ARX benchmarks

**Graduate Research Assistant**, Logistics and Distribution Institute, University of Louisville, August 2021 to July 2023

- Multi-task Cox proportional hazards model quantifying transplantation-center-level factors affecting one-year post-heart-transplant survivability, each center treated as a separate learning task over a shared representation
- Integrated national registry donor and recipient records with county- and tract-level area deprivation data within a 200-mile radius of each center, benchmarked against CoxPH and non-linear CoxPH by concordance index
- Authored five modules for an NSF-funded workforce training program on cybersecurity in logistics systems
