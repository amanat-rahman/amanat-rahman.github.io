## Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology

Department of Mechanical and Production Engineering. Lecturer, 2015 to 2018; Assistant Professor, 2018 to 2021. Instructor of record, theory sections of approximately 50 students.

**Operations research and production systems**

- **IPE 3107: Operations Research.**
  Topics: linear programming and the simplex method, duality and sensitivity analysis, transportation and assignment models, network flows, integer programming.
- **IPE 3205: Production Planning and Control.**
  Topics: demand forecasting, aggregate planning, master production scheduling, MRP, inventory models, line balancing, shop-floor scheduling.
- **IPE 4105: Supply Chain Management.**
  Topics: network design, sourcing and procurement, distribution and logistics, inventory pooling, bullwhip effect, coordination and contracts.
- **IPE 4103: Operations and Project Management.**
  Topics: operations strategy, work breakdown structures, CPM and PERT, crashing and resource levelling, project risk and quality planning.
- **IPE 4206: Industrial Simulation** (laboratory).
  Topics: discrete-event simulation modeling, input distribution fitting, verification and validation, output analysis, scenario comparison.

**Quantitative methods and engineering economics**

- **MATH 2207: Numerical Analysis and Statistics.**
  Topics: root finding, numerical integration and differentiation, curve fitting and interpolation, probability distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, regression.
- **HUM 2107: Engineering Economy.**
  Topics: time value of money, equivalence, present and annual worth, rate of return, replacement and depreciation, benefit-cost evaluation.

**Manufacturing, design, and laboratory instruction**

## Proposed courses

- **Optimization Modeling for Engineering Decisions** (undergraduate). Project-based; students formulate, solve, and defend an optimization model for a decision problem they select, working in solver environments from the first week.
- **Stochastic Programming and Decomposition Methods** (graduate). Two-stage and multi-stage formulations, scenario generation and reduction, the L-shaped method and its integer extension, Benders decomposition. Students build a working decomposition solver over the term.
