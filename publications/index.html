2026

  1. Under Review
    Using Stochastic Programming to Mitigate Patient No-Shows and Short-Notice Cancellations in Outpatient Clinics through Overbooking and Walk-Ins
    Amanat Ur Rahman, Tommaso Giovannelli, Karmel S. Shehadeh, and 1 more author
    Working paper, 2026
    Abs

    A two-stage stochastic program with an integer first stage that jointly determines overbooking levels and appointment slot assignments under uncertain no-shows, short-notice cancellations, and walk-in arrivals. The model is solved by an integer L-shaped decomposition embedded in a branch-and-cut framework.

  2. In Prep.
    Allocating Open-Access Appointment Slots in a Single-Provider Session: A Two-Stage Stochastic Multi-Objective Program with an Integer First Stage
    Amanat Ur Rahman and Tommaso Giovannelli
    In preparation, 2026
    Abs

    A multi-objective extension of the outpatient scheduling problem cast within a stochastic set-optimization framework, characterizing the Pareto frontier across competing patient-centered and provider-centered objectives.
