Proposed courses

  • Optimization Modeling for Engineering Decisions (undergraduate)
    • Project-based; students formulate, solve, and defend an optimization model for a decision problem they select
    • Solver environments from the first week, with emphasis on formulation and on interpreting a solution for a non-technical stakeholder
  • Stochastic Programming and Decomposition Methods (graduate)
    • Two-stage and multi-stage formulations, scenario generation and reduction, the L-shaped method and its integer extension, Benders decomposition
    • Taught through implementation; students build a working decomposition solver over the term
